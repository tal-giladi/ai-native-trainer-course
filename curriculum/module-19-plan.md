# Module 19 plan — AI Adoption and Change Management

4 lessons · ~60 min instruction · ~4 h practice · depends on Module 12 (Fabrikam: 400 developers, gateway, per-team keys, ADRs, requirements R1–R10), Module 11 (11.5 governance: owners, change classes, the system-evolution loop, cadence), Module 16 (Kirkpatrick levels, show-do-reflect, measuring learning, follow-up with Wilson), Module 17 (workshop as the developer training path; 17.3 answer shape for objections), Module 13 (delivery metrics, evidence base, claims ladder), Module 3 (03.2 portable AI layer, AGENTS.md). Module 18 (written in parallel) is linked by manifest path for the questions log and lunch-and-learn.

Thesis: a platform that passed its security review can still fail, because adoption is a change in many people's weekly habits, decided person by person (usefulness, effort, social influence, facilitating conditions) inside an organisation that either makes it safe and worth it or does not. The student plans that change for Fabrikam's 400 developers: stakeholders and their objections answered with evidence rather than promises, a network of sanctioned peer champions and one default tool, enablement whose owners are employees, and metrics that notice a slide in a week. The break for the whole module is the outline's sample: three months after the engagement, usage is back to 10%.

Shared lab: `labs/module-19/` — `tools/AdoptCheck` (dependency-free C#: `stakeholders`, `champions`, `enablement`, `plan`, `usage`), `fabrikam/brief.md` (waves, countries, eleven stakeholder quotes), `solution/` (reference plan v1 + 26 weeks of illustrative usage and events), `break/` (one flawed piece per lesson; 19.4 has the first rollout's usage falling from 63% to 9%), `scripts/generate_usage.py`.
