- **Trainer** — Sells learning: people can do something afterwards; judged by measured learning gain and later behavior change, not satisfaction (lessons/module-01/lesson-01.md)
- **Consultant** — Sells an outcome: an engineering metric that moves; judged by a before/after number with an interval (lessons/module-01/lesson-01.md)
- **Enablement engineer** — Internal owner of the AI layer and agent platform; judged by sustained, trusted adoption and fewer incidents (lessons/module-01/lesson-01.md)
- **Flywheel** — The four-stage order practitioner → public teaching → community → private engagements, where each stage produces the proof the next one needs (lessons/module-01/lesson-01.md)
- **Proof chain** — Each sentence you want to say to a client, mapped to the artifact that makes it checkable and the module that produces it (lessons/module-01/lesson-01.md)
- **Pain** — A problem that is observable, recurring, costly and owned by someone with authority; strongest when a workaround or abandoned attempt exists (lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- **Hype** — A solution-first, tool-first or fear-driven request with no event, metric or owner, e.g. "we need AI training" (lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- **Symptom** — An observable signal, such as low seat usage, whose underlying pain is not yet known (lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- **Blocker** — A stakeholder or constraint that can stop the work (CISO, DBA lead, legal); designed for early, not solved (lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- **Buyer / user / champion** — Who signs and owns budget; who lives the pain daily; who wants it to succeed and carries evidence into rooms you are not in (lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- **Effort vs flow** — Engineer-hours of work (bounded by team capacity) versus calendar delay (median, p90); never multiplied into each other when sizing a pain (lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- **Wedge** — A one-sentence positioning of stack × audience × pain narrow enough that a stranger can tell whether they are in or out (lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)
- **Stack inventory** — The platforms the wedge audience actually runs (language, database, tracker, docs, code host, CI) that labs and integrations are built against (lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)
- **Reachability funnel** — Contacts × share matching the wedge × reply rate, estimated from your own network rather than by multiplying correlated survey shares (lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)
- **Customer discovery interview** — A non-selling conversation about specific past events to learn whether a pain is real, who feels it and who pays (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- **Leading question** — A question that contains or suggests its answer ("don't you find…"), producing agreement instead of information (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- **Evidence ladder** — Ranking of interview answers from compliments and future promises up to specific past events, workarounds and commitments (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- **Commitment (discovery)** — Something a participant spends now — an introduction, data, a second session — as opposed to a compliment or a promise (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- **Pain/problem map** — Table of pains backed by past events, with quotes, costs, workarounds and stakeholders; problem statements only for pains heard in two or more independent interviews (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- **Saturation** — The point in qualitative interviewing where new interviews stop producing new themes (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
