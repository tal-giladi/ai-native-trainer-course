- **Productized service** — A service sold at one price for one defined scope, delivered with standard assets so that hours fall and vary less with each delivery; between consulting and a product (lessons/module-22/lesson-01.md)
- **Service card** — One page defining a productized service: price, duration, entry criteria, steps with owners and standard assets, a budgeted custom step, exclusions and a version (lessons/module-22/lesson-01.md)
- **Effective day rate** — A fixed fee divided by the days actually worked on it; compared with your cost floor to see whether a delivery paid (lessons/module-22/lesson-01.md)
- **Learning curve (Wright's law)** — The model $T_n = T_1 n^{-b}$ in which hours per unit fall by a constant share each time cumulative output doubles; learning rate $2^{-b}$ (lessons/module-22/lesson-01.md)
- **Revenue per live day** — Monthly revenue divided by the live days it needs, including fixed days such as community office hours; a measure of how much a catalog scales beyond your calendar (lessons/module-22/lesson-02.md)
- **Team licence** — Written terms letting one company use a course or kit internally, stating named seats, term, resale or sublicensing, attribution and which updates are included (lessons/module-22/lesson-02.md)
- **Creative Commons licence** — One of six public licences (BY, BY-SA, BY-ND, BY-NC, BY-NC-SA, BY-NC-ND) for creative works; CC recommends against them for software (lessons/module-22/lesson-02.md)
- **Community rules** — The terms that make a paid community a bounded product: response time, office hours, exclusions, moderation and what happens if it closes (lessons/module-22/lesson-02.md)
- **Kit manifest** — The file listing every item of a template or starter kit with version, licence, last-verified date and stranger-test result, plus the kit's version and support policy (lessons/module-22/lesson-03.md)
- **Changelog** — A human-readable, newest-first record of notable changes per release (Added, Changed, Deprecated, Removed, Fixed, Security), as in Keep a Changelog (lessons/module-22/lesson-03.md)
- **Item difficulty** — The share of learners who answer an assessment item correctly, $p$ (lessons/module-22/lesson-03.md)
- **Discrimination index** — For an assessment item, $D = p_U - p_L$, the difference in correct rates between the top and bottom 27% of learners by total score; negative values suggest a miskeyed or misleading item (lessons/module-22/lesson-03.md)
- **Certificate of completion** — A document stating that a learner attended, did the exercises and took the assessment, without claiming job competence; contrasted with a credential, which needs validity evidence and a defended cut score (lessons/module-22/lesson-03.md)
- **Capacity cap** — The stated number of days a month you sell, kept in practice and published in the application form, with a rule for what happens when it is full (lessons/module-22/lesson-04.md)
- **Capped retainer** — A monthly retainer with a day cap, expiry of unused days, a notice period and a scope, priced per capped day (lessons/module-22/lesson-04.md)
- **Utilization** — Demand divided by capacity for a resource such as your delivery days; waiting time grows with $\rho/(1-\rho)$ as it approaches 100% (lessons/module-22/lesson-04.md)
- **Kingman's approximation** — $W_q \approx \frac{\rho}{1-\rho}\cdot\frac{c_a^2+c_s^2}{2}\cdot\tau$, the expected wait in a single-server queue from utilization, arrival and service variability and mean service time (lessons/module-22/lesson-04.md)
- **Stop rule** — A pre-committed condition with a number and a date under which a product or activity stops, written before sunk cost can decide (lessons/module-22/lesson-04.md)
- **Productization plan** — The 12-month plan that fits a capacity cap: allocation, retainers, queue check, revenue streams as ranges, roadmap with exit signals, and stop rules (lessons/module-22/lesson-04.md)
