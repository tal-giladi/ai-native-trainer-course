- **Brownfield demo** — A purpose-built, confidentiality-safe legacy repository with real history, hidden conventions, a company around it and a tagged before/after, used for every public demo instead of employer code (lessons/module-15/lesson-01.md)
- **Demo ticket** — The one ticket run live, chosen from at least ten measured runs for repeatability, a visible before/after gap, duration and recognizability (lessons/module-15/lesson-01.md)
- **Deny list** — A private list of employer, client, product and colleague names scanned against every commit of a public repository; kept outside that repository (lessons/module-15/lesson-01.md)
- **Company scaffold** — The fictional organization around a demo repository: handbook, team, glossary, PRDs, tickets in a tracker and at least one stale page (lessons/module-15/lesson-02.md)
- **Before/after tags** — Two tags on the demo repository, one with an empty AI layer and one with the layer, between which only AI-layer paths change and the demo ticket stays open (lessons/module-15/lesson-02.md)
- **Unedited recording** — One take of the full loop on the demo ticket, with no pauses, cuts or speed-ups, used as rehearsal data, fallback material and public evidence (lessons/module-15/lesson-03.md)
- **Off-script prompt** — A prompt the presenter improvised during a run; evidence of a step the AI layer or the run sheet is missing (lessons/module-15/lesson-03.md)
- **Stranger test** — A developer who has never seen the AI layer tries to get a working PR from it in 30 minutes, thinking aloud, with zero help; any hint ends the test (lessons/module-15/lesson-03.md)
- **Think-aloud protocol** — A usability method in which participants verbalize what they look for and expect while the facilitator stays silent (lessons/module-15/lesson-03.md)
- **Run sheet** — The one-page plan for a live demo: segments with mode, time budget, rehearsal count, fallback, recovery budget and the sentence to say when it breaks (lessons/module-15/lesson-04.md)
- **Pre-baked fallback** — A branch or tag holding the finished result of a live segment, switched to when the segment fails or overruns (lessons/module-15/lesson-04.md)
- **P(clean)** — The chance a live demo runs without any failure, estimated as the product of per-segment rehearsal success rates (lessons/module-15/lesson-04.md)
- **Demo drill** — A rehearsal in which a randomly chosen failure is induced in a live segment to practise recovery, after chaos engineering (lessons/module-15/lesson-04.md)
