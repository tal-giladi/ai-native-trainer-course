- **Methodology** — A claim about how a kind of work goes wrong, a repeatable way of doing it better and the evidence that it works, packaged so others can apply it: problem, loop, concepts, evidence, boundaries, artifacts, evolution (lessons/module-14/lesson-01.md)
- **Semantic diffusion** — The weakening of a term's meaning as it spreads to people who never read its definition (Fowler); countered by bounded definitions that someone keeps restating (lessons/module-14/lesson-01.md)
- **Tool-change test** — Share of a method's sentences that only make sense for one tool or release; above about 20%, a tool change breaks the method (lessons/module-14/lesson-01.md)
- **Concept (in a method)** — A named, bounded, falsifiable claim about how agent work fails or succeeds, traced to dated incidents or experiments (lessons/module-14/lesson-02.md)
- **Concept card** — The record of one concept: status, date named, plain-words definition, claim, boundary, counter-evidence and dated evidence trail (lessons/module-14/lesson-02.md)
- **Counter-evidence** — An observation that would contradict a concept's claim; every card states what would count (lessons/module-14/lesson-02.md)
- **Paraphrase test** — The module's exit test: a non-engineer reads the loop and concepts, then explains them back without the page (lessons/module-14/lesson-02.md)
- **Method versioning** — MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH applied to what people learned: loop steps and concept names are the public interface; retire, never delete; never reuse a name (lessons/module-14/lesson-03.md)
- **Evolution policy (method)** — One page saying what triggers a review of the method, who decides and how teaching materials follow; distinct from the AI-layer governance policy (lessons/module-14/lesson-03.md)
- **Objections FAQ** — Hard objections in the asker's words, each answered from evidence or with an explicit statement of what is not known (lessons/module-14/lesson-03.md)
- **Attribution audit** — A check of every borrowed term, shape, diagram and example against public frameworks, with a decision for each: attribute, replace or justify (lessons/module-14/lesson-04.md)
- **Original synthesis** — A method that credits the shapes it shares with others and adds its own named concepts, evidence and a stated contribution; the opposite of a copied framework (lessons/module-14/lesson-04.md)
- **Idea/expression distinction** — Copyright protects how something is expressed (text, slides, diagrams), not ideas, methods, systems or short phrases (lessons/module-14/lesson-04.md)
