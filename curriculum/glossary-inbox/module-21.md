- **engagement record** — The private, phase-by-phase record of one client engagement: stakeholders, acceptors, audit, baseline, decisions, PR log, owners, results and follow-ups; the only source a case study may draw on (lessons/module-21/lesson-01.md)
- **stakeholder map** — A table of the people who can stop, slow or accept an engagement, with influence, interest, worries, success in their words and a plan for each; built from interviews before kickoff (lessons/module-21/lesson-01.md)
- **premortem** — A planning exercise in which the team assumes the project has already failed and writes down why, so that risks get early signals and owners while they are cheap (lessons/module-21/lesson-01.md)
- **working agreement** — The operational layer under a SOW: named acceptors per deliverable, dated access, data handling, cadence, channel and escalation (lessons/module-21/lesson-01.md)
- **minimum detectable effect** — The smallest true change a comparison of a given size and spread would detect with the chosen significance and power; for log cycle times, $\delta = (z_{1-\alpha/2}+z_{1-\beta})\sigma\sqrt{2/n}$ (lessons/module-21/lesson-02.md)
- **baseline** — The before-state of every outcome an engagement will measure, captured from history before the build starts, with definitions, a spread and a quality guardrail (lessons/module-21/lesson-02.md)
- **enabling stance** — Helping a team gain a capability temporarily and then moving on, rather than building on its behalf (after Team Topologies' enabling teams) (lessons/module-21/lesson-03.md)
- **client-authored share** — The fraction of AI-layer changes in a period whose author is a client employee; in the last third of a build it should be at least 70% (lessons/module-21/lesson-03.md)
- **truck factor** — The minimal number of people whose departure would leave more than half of a codebase's files with nobody who has worked on them; computed for an engagement over client people only (lessons/module-21/lesson-03.md)
- **handover readiness** — The condition for leaving: every recurring activity has an employee owner and a backup who has already run it once without the consultant (lessons/module-21/lesson-04.md)
- **follow-up** — A short, scoped check 30, 60 and 90 days after handover that each owned rhythm ran and that usage per team is within its control limits (lessons/module-21/lesson-04.md)
- **motivated intruder test** — Asking whether a competent person with public information and a reason to try could identify the subject of published material; the standard for anonymizing a case study (lessons/module-21/lesson-05.md)
- **case study** — A one-page, anonymized, client-approved account of one engagement whose every number and status comes from the engagement record, with limitations as a section (lessons/module-21/lesson-05.md)
