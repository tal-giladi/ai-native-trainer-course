- **Change management** — The planned work of moving people from current to new ways of working: stakeholders, champions, enablement, measurement and the mechanisms that keep the change after its sponsors move on (lessons/module-19/lesson-01.md)
- **Adoption funnel** — The stages from seats assigned to activated, weekly active and engaged use; habitual users are the product of the stage conversion rates (lessons/module-19/lesson-01.md)
- **UTAUT** — Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology: performance expectancy, effort expectancy, social influence and facilitating conditions as determinants of whether people use a system (lessons/module-19/lesson-01.md)
- **Stakeholder map** — A table of every group whose support, time or signature a rollout needs, with influence, stance, objection, evidence-backed answer, what changes for them and an owner (lessons/module-19/lesson-01.md)
- **Works council** — Elected employee representation in some countries; in Germany it co-determines the introduction of technical systems designed to monitor employees' behaviour or performance (lessons/module-19/lesson-01.md)
- **Opinion leader** — A person whose informal influence over peers' adoption decisions comes from competence, accessibility and similarity to them; the route through which diffusion reaches the majority (lessons/module-19/lesson-02.md)
- **Champion** — A peer inside one team with sanctioned hours to make the agent useful for that team: first responder, local examples, team rules owner, feedback conduit, practice host (lessons/module-19/lesson-02.md)
- **Tool fragmentation** — Supporting several agent tools at once, which multiplies security reviews, gateway routes, rules formats and training paths; measured by the effective number of tools $1/H$ (lessons/module-19/lesson-02.md)
- **Effective number of tools** — The reciprocal of the concentration index $H = \sum s_i^2$ of tool shares; 1 for a single tool, $k$ for $k$ equally used tools (lessons/module-19/lesson-02.md)
- **Enablement** — The recurring mechanisms that make a tool usable in practice: role-based training, practice time, office hours, a feedback loop and ownership (lessons/module-19/lesson-03.md)
- **Office hours** — A short, scheduled session where people bring real stuck tasks; questions are logged and turned into FAQ entries and feedback items (lessons/module-19/lesson-03.md)
- **Little's law** — $L = \lambda W$: in a stable queue, the average number of open items equals the arrival rate times the average time in the system (lessons/module-19/lesson-03.md)
- **AI literacy** — The skills, knowledge and understanding to use AI systems in an informed way; EU AI Act Article 4 requires providers and deployers to take measures to support it for their staff (lessons/module-19/lesson-03.md)
- **Weekly active share** — Share of seats with at least one agent session in a week; the main habit metric of adoption (lessons/module-19/lesson-04.md)
- **p-chart** — A control chart for proportions with limits $\bar p \pm 3\sqrt{\bar p(1-\bar p)/n}$; a share below the lower limit is unlikely to be ordinary variation (lessons/module-19/lesson-04.md)
- **Adoption regression** — Usage falling back after a rollout, as a step (something changed), a decay (support and habit fading), a measurement gap or a cohort that never started (lessons/module-19/lesson-04.md)
- **Handover criteria** — The conditions under which an external consultant can leave: the internal owner has run every recurring mechanism unaided and the alerting has been tested (lessons/module-19/lesson-04.md)
