# Productization plan — year 2 (2026-11 to 2027-10)

> **Illustrative.** One student's plan after three paid pilots, with a full-time job and the employer's written permission. It ties together the [service card](service-pilot.md), the [catalog](catalog.md) and the [practitioner kit](practitioner-kit/kit.md). Prices are placeholders (USD, as of 2026-09); legal lines are **orientation only**. Format: [productization plan template](../../../templates/productization-plan.md). Check with `ScaleCheck plan`.

- Owner: the student, sole proprietor
- Start: 2026-11
- Capacity: 8 days a month, the same cap as the offer ladder (20.1); weekends and leave, not evenings after work
- Floor: 1,500 per day (pricing worksheet, 20.2)
- Employer: written permission of 2026-09-10 covers training, consulting and training products; no employer customers, competitors or suppliers; reviewed again at month 6
- When full: new pilot requests get an honest start date from this plan; if that is more than 3 months away, I refer them to two named peers; the application form states the cap
- Demand: 0.15–0.25 pilots a month (two to three a year, from last year's six qualified applications)
- Job days: 10
- Job variability: 0.16 (CV of delivery hours, service card)
- Arrival variability: 1.0 (applications arrive at random)

## Stop rules

- If fewer than 10 course seats are sold by month 7, stop recording new modules and sell the course only as pre-work for the workshop.
- If the community has fewer than 12 paying members for 2 months in a row, close it with 60 days' notice, as its rules promise.
- If any month uses more than 8 days, take nothing new the following month.
- If a pilot takes more than 100 hours twice in a row, the service card goes back for a scope and price review.

## What I will not do this year

- Hire or subcontract delivery: the method is not yet written down well enough for someone else to deliver it (the stranger test for the Bound plan is 5 of 6).
- Sell a credential. The course ends with a certificate of completion.
- Leave the job. The exit signal from the source path, product revenue that is "non-trivial" next to workshop revenue without more live hours, is checked at month 12 against this plan's numbers, not before.
