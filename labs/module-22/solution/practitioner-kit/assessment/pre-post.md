# Pre/post assessment — v1.1.0

> Part of the practitioner kit. **Not public**: licensed to buyers of the workshop, the course and the team licence for use with their own learners. Format: [pre/post assessment template](../../../../../templates/pre-post-assessment.md) (Module 16).

- Forms: A and B, parallel, 10 multiple-choice items each, mapped to objectives O1–O4 of the workshop agenda
- Use: form A before, form B after (or the reverse for half the group); report the class normalized gain with a bootstrap interval (16.5)
- What a score means: how well a learner can spot a shadow rule or a self-graded test in a diff and choose the right Ground and Bound steps, on the fictional starter pack, on the day
- What it does not mean: that the learner will apply it on their own codebase, or that their team will ship faster. The completion document says "certificate of completion", not a credential.

## Item analysis

Run `ScaleCheck items` on every new batch of answers (at least 20 learners). Latest: `responses.csv`, 24 learners from three workshops, form A v1.1.0: all ten items with difficulty between 0.29 and 0.79 and discrimination of at least 0.50. Item 4 of v1.0.0 had negative discrimination and was removed in 1.2.0 (see CHANGELOG).

## Items

The items and the key are in the private kit repository; this page is the manifest. Each item records its objective, its key, the misconception each distractor represents, and the version in which it last changed.
