## Community rules

- Response time: questions answered within 3 working days; anything longer goes to office hours
- Office hours: 2 sessions of 60 minutes a month, recorded for members only, with consent at the start
- Not included: reviews of members' private code, client-specific advice, anything that needs an NDA; those are a pilot or a paid call
- Moderation: code of conduct; no employer or client code or data posted; posts with secrets removed within a day
- If it closes: 60 days' notice, pro-rata refund of the current month, members can export their own posts

## Why this order

The free starter pack and posts earn trust; the workshop is still the flagship and the best evidence; the course and the team licence sell the workshop's content without my hours; the community is where next year's material comes from. The course earns a lower gain than the live workshop (0.41 vs 0.57 on the same parallel forms, different people), and the course page says so.
