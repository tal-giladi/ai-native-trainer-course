# Exercise — hype or pain?

For [01.2](../../../lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md). Twelve things people said to a trainer in the first month of looking for a wedge (fictional, composited). Classify each as one of:

- **Pain** — passes the four tests: observable, recurring, costly, owned.
- **Hype** — solution-first, tool-first or fear-driven; no event, no metric, no owner.
- **Symptom** — a real signal, but the pain behind it is not yet known. Your next question is the point.
- **Blocker** — a constraint someone can use to stop the work. Not a pain you solve; a condition you design for.

For each one write: the class, which of the four tests it passes, and the one follow-up question you would ask next. Then check against [the key](../solutions/hype-or-pain-key.md).
