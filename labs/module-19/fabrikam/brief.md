# Fabrikam Group — brief for the agent rollout

*Hypothetical enterprise from [Module 12](../../module-12/fabrikam/brief.md). Any resemblance to a real company is accidental. People, numbers and events are illustrative.*

## Where Module 12 left it

The platform is designed and has passed security review: EU-hosted models behind a central gateway with per-team keys and quotas (ADR-0001, ADR-0006, ADR-0007), short-lived identities (ADR-0003), 30-day content logs and 400-day audit metadata (ADR-0004), EU residency including fallback (ADR-0005). The ADRs are in [`labs/module-12/solution/adr/`](../../module-12/solution/adr/0001-eu-hosted-cloud-models.md). None of that makes a single developer use it.

## The organisation

- 400 developers in eight teams: billing 48, payments 64, identity 36, lending 72, mobile 56, data 40, platform 34, web 50.
- Developers are employed in Germany (about 55%), the Netherlands (about 25%) and Israel (about 20%). The German entity has a works council.
- The pilot (March to June 2026) used 30 volunteers from billing, payments and platform. The pilot survey was positive; the volunteers were, by construction, the people who wanted it.
- Rollout in three waves: billing, payments, platform in week 1; identity, data, web in week 3; lending and mobile in week 5. About two new hires join each week from week 13.
- Sponsor: R. Vogel, CTO. Platform owner: Eitan Shaked. Enablement lead (to be hired or named): see the plan.

## What people said (stakeholder interviews, illustrative)

| Who | What they said |
|---|---|
| Developer, lending (later wave) | "It writes something that is almost right and I spend longer fixing it than writing it." |
| Senior engineer, payments | "Juniors will merge code nobody on the team understands, and I'll be the one reviewing it." |
| Developer, web | "Everyone knows what this is really for. First the tool, then the headcount review." |
| Team lead, mobile | "My sprints are full. Show me it pays before you take two hours a week from my people." |
| Director of engineering | "The board wants 100% adoption by Q4. Can we just make it mandatory?" |
| CISO office | "An agent with repo, Jira and database access is an exfiltration path with a friendly face." |
| Legal counsel | "Two questions: does the provider train on our code, and who owns what it writes?" |
| Data protection officer | "Your telemetry has user e-mail on every metric. What is it for, and who sees it?" |
| Works council chair (DE) | "Any system that can show who uses it how much is a system that can monitor performance. We co-decide on that." |
| Finance business partner | "We are paying for 400 seats from day one. What happens to seats nobody uses?" |
| Procurement | "Mobile's vendor offers Tool B in a bundle for free. Why not use it?" |

## Data you get

- `usage.csv` — weekly counts per team from the gateway: `week, team, cohort, seats, active, engaged`. Active = at least one agent session that week; engaged = sessions on at least 3 days that week. Team-level only.
- `events.csv` — what happened in which week.

The break data ([`break/19.4-regression/`](../break/19.4-regression/events.csv)) is the first rollout, run with a draft plan by a previous consultant. The solution data ([`solution/`](../solution/adoption-plan.md)) is the same waves under the reference plan. Both are generated by `scripts/generate_usage.py`.
