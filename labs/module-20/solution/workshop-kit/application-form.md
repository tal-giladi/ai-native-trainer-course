# Application form — Ground before you generate

> **Illustrative.** The student's copy of the [application form template](../../../../templates/application-form.md). Answers land in [`applications.csv`](applications.csv); `OfferCheck qualify` scores them.

**Before you apply.** I help legacy .NET and SQL Server teams that already use a coding agent get PRs that pass review, and measure on their own tickets whether it helped. I take at most one pilot or implementation at a time and two workshops a month (8 working days a month in total). I do not promise productivity figures, and I never need access to production systems. I do not work for customers, competitors or suppliers of my employer. I answer every application within five working days.

1. Your role and your team's size.
2. In one or two sentences, what problem do you want to solve?
3. Tell me about the last time it happened: what happened, and what did it cost?
4. Who would sponsor this, and who decides? (roles, not names)
5. Is there a budget for this? (approved / we know the process / none yet / don't know)
6. When would you want to start, in weeks from now?
7. Does the team use a coding agent today? (daily / weekly / no)
8. What would a good result look like for you?
9. Is your organization a customer, competitor or supplier of [my employer's name]? (yes / no / not sure)
10. Your name and work email, so I can reply.

*Privacy.* I use your answers only to decide whether and how I can help, keep them for 12 months, and delete them on request. I do not share them.
