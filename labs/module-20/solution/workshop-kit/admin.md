# Engagement admin register

> **Illustrative, orientation only.** One student's register for their first paid engagements while still employed. The answers are fictional and jurisdiction-dependent; the structure is the point. Every "yes" points to a document or a dated email, and the blockers were cleared before the Fabrikam SOW was signed. Check with `OfferCheck admin`.

- Jurisdiction: supplier in the student's home country; client in another; both named in the contract's governing-law clause
- Reviewed by: an employment lawyer (E1–E3, 2026-09-02) and an accountant (T1, I1, 2026-09-09), both local to the student
- Payment terms: net 30
