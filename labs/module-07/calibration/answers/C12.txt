ADR 0007 (April 2024) replaced SqlHelper with Dapper repositories after untyped DataSets caused incidents, but one class, MonthlyRevenueReport, still uses it, and BILL-97 will migrate it. That is the only reason the helper still exists, which is why it carries an Obsolete attribute. When you need data access, add a method to a repository interface like IInvoiceRepository and implement it with Dapper and parameterized SQL, taking a CancellationToken. Do not call SqlHelper yourself; ConventionTests will fail the build if you do outside Legacy.
