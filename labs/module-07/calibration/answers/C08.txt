SqlHelper remains only for MonthlyRevenueReport, which is its last caller. The platform team scheduled its removal for Q3 under ticket BILL-79, after which the Legacy folder will be deleted entirely. Until then, new data access must use a Dapper repository behind an interface as described in ADR 0007, with parameterized SQL and cancellation tokens. ConventionTests will fail if you call SqlHelper outside Legacy. Please do not extend it.
