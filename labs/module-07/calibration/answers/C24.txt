Two reasons it is still in the repository: MonthlyRevenueReport, its last caller, has not been migrated yet, and that migration belongs to BILL-97 rather than to whoever happens to touch the file. Everything else moved to Dapper repositories after ADR 0007, because untyped DataSets had caused production incidents. When you need data access, add a method to an interface like IInvoiceRepository and implement it with Dapper, parameterized SQL and a CancellationToken. Do not call SqlHelper from new code; the convention tests will fail the build.
