The helper survives only because MonthlyRevenueReport, the last caller, has not been migrated; BILL-97 tracks that work and nobody should do it as a drive-by. For your own code, follow ADR 0007: a Dapper repository behind an interface, modelled on IInvoiceRepository and InvoiceRepository. Keep SQL parameterized and pass a CancellationToken through every async method. SqlHelper is Obsolete, and the convention tests fail if anything outside Legacy calls it.
