# Evolution policy — Ground-Bound-Build-Prove (reference, illustrative)

> Reference for lesson 14.3, filled in from the [method evolution policy template](../../../templates/method-evolution-policy.md). One page.

**Scope.** `method-vN.md`, `concepts.md`, `diagrams.md` share one version. `implementation-YYYY-MM.md` is outside the version and reviewed quarterly.

**Versions.** MAJOR when a loop step or a taught concept changes meaning, is retired or renamed without an alias; MINOR when a concept is added, promoted or renamed with its old name kept; PATCH for wording and new evidence that does not change a status. Retired names are never reused. `MethodCheck diff` runs on every change.

**Triggers.**

1. Counter-evidence: any incident where a concept's claim did not hold — reviewed within two weeks, logged on the card.
2. A new experiment report — reviewed before the next workshop; every number in the method is checked against it.
3. A major agent or model change — `implementation-*.md` updated; every claim with a number is marked "measured on [agent version]" until re-measured.
4. An audience objection I could not answer with evidence — added to `faq.md`; decision within a month.
5. A new public framework covering the same ground — attribution audit rerun.
6. Otherwise quarterly (first Monday of Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct).

**People.** Owner: me. MAJOR changes are reviewed by the team lead who saw the incidents; the review is a 30-minute walk through the evidence IDs in the changelog entry.

**Materials.** A MAJOR change lists the slides, exercises and posts it breaks. Slides and exercises are updated before the next delivery. Published posts are not rewritten; the method page says which version each post describes.

**Example.** 2.0.0 (2026-09-14): Harden added after INC-09 and INC-13 were both fixed by checks, not rules; C4 retired into it; slides 3, 7, 12 and the loop diagram updated; the June lunch-and-learn post links to the 1.0.0 page.
