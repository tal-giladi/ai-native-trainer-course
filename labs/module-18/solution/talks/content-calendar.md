## Rules this calendar follows

1. One piece, one concept from `concepts.md`, with the evidence IDs it rests on. A piece that teaches nothing from the method is not in the calendar.
2. At most one piece in five asks for anything (a seat, a call, a download).
3. Every published piece has a link. Internal talks are *delivered*, with the deck linked.
4. Reach is recorded, but the column that matters is **Outside questions**: questions from people who did not know the student before, asked without being prompted. The questions themselves go to [`questions.csv`](questions.csv).
5. A theme asked three times becomes a piece (P-06 from `model-improves`, P-08 from `no-test-suite`).
