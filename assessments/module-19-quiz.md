# Module 19 quiz — AI adoption and change management

Nine scenario questions on why technically sound AI rollouts fail organisationally and what keeps them alive: reading stakeholders and their objections, building a champion network and a tool strategy, designing enablement with owners and capacity, and measuring adoption well enough to catch and diagnose a regression after the consultant has left. They test reasoning, not recall: most describe a plan, a conversation or a set of usage numbers and ask what is wrong or what to do next.

**Covers**

- [19.1 · Why rollouts fail](../lessons/module-19/lesson-01.md) — acceptance determinants, the adoption funnel, failure modes, stakeholder map, concede–evidence–boundary answers, employee representation.
- [19.2 · Champions and tool fragmentation](../lessons/module-19/lesson-02.md) — adopter categories and opinion leaders, sanctioned champions with succession, concentration and effective number of tools, default plus ADR exceptions.
- [19.3 · Enablement mechanics](../lessons/module-19/lesson-03.md) — role-based paths, practice time, office hours, the feedback loop and Little's law, acceptable use and AI literacy, owners who stay.
- [19.4 · Measuring adoption and preventing regression](../lessons/module-19/lesson-04.md) — reach, habit and outcome metrics, team-level aggregation, p-chart limits, step vs decay, anti-regression mechanisms, handover criteria.

**Pass mark:** 70% (7 of 9). Unlimited retries; answers and explanations appear after you submit.
