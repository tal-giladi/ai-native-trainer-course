# Module 18 quiz — Teaching in public

Nine scenario questions on building credibility in public: planning content as a funnel measured by the right signal, writing claims your evidence supports, choosing and preparing formats, and turning live sessions and audience questions into the next pieces. They test reasoning, not recall: most describe a calendar, a draft, a script or a questions log and ask what is wrong or what to do next.

**Covers**

- [18.1 · Content as a funnel](../lessons/module-18/lesson-01.md) — discover, trust and act stages, audience research, credibility as a track record, attention vs signal, $1-(1-p)^n$ for pieces until a stranger asks.
- [18.2 · Evidence-based technical writing](../lessons/module-18/lesson-02.md) — the claims ladder in posts, source and interval in the same sentence, substantiation and "studies show", hype propagation, limits and disclosure, plain language.
- [18.3 · Content formats](../lessons/module-18/lesson-03.md) — formats by job, cost and proof, one concept per short piece, video length and openings, captions, pinned demo repositories, case-study pieces.
- [18.4 · Lunch-and-learn, free pilot, and questions into content](../lessons/module-18/lesson-04.md) — the internal session, the free pilot run like a paid engagement, consent, the questions log, the exit signal.

**Pass mark:** 70% (7 of 9). Unlimited retries; answers and explanations appear after you submit.

Before you start, it helps to have run the checks in [`labs/module-18`](../labs/module-18/README.md) on the break files.
