# Module 10 quiz — Multi-agent systems

Nine scenario questions on designing, debugging and judging multi-agent systems for a Contoso Billing AI layer: topologies, handoffs and shared files, review loops and their cost, and the comparison that decides whether any of it beats one agent. They test reasoning, not recall: most describe a pipeline, a trace or a result and ask what it shows, what went wrong, or what to measure next.

**Covers**

- [10.1 · Multi-agent topologies](../lessons/module-10/lesson-01.md) — four mechanisms, six topologies, choosing by task properties, implicit decisions.
- [10.2 · Coordination: handoffs, shared state and conflicts](../lessons/module-10/lesson-02.md) — handoff contracts, ownership and leases, merge policies, precedence.
- [10.3 · Cost, latency and failure propagation](../lessons/module-10/lesson-03.md) — expected cost and rounds, critical path, $\prod p_i$, the reviewer equation, traces and stop conditions.
- [10.4 · When multi-agent is worse](../lessons/module-10/lesson-04.md) — the fair comparison, the compute-matched baseline, reporting and recommending.

**Pass mark:** 70% (7 of 9). Unlimited retries; answers and explanations appear after you submit.

Before you start, it helps to have done the labs in [`labs/module-10`](../labs/module-10/README.md) — several questions use `AgentTeam` and `EvalHarness` output.
