# Module 15 quiz — The demo repo and live demo craft

Ten scenario questions on building a public demo of AI-native work and running it live: what makes a brownfield demo credible, keeping it safe to publish, choosing the demo ticket, making the before/after a fair comparison, recording an unedited run, running the stranger test, and recovering when the demo breaks. They test reasoning, not recall: most describe a repository, a run or a run sheet and ask what is wrong with it or what to do next.

**Covers**

- [15.1 · Designing a credible brownfield demo](../lessons/module-15/lesson-01.md) — the six properties, choosing the demo ticket on evidence, confidentiality across the whole history.
- [15.2 · Company scaffolding and the before state](../lessons/module-15/lesson-02.md) — the company around the code, the before/after as a controlled comparison, what one run shows.
- [15.3 · The unedited recording and the stranger test](../lessons/module-15/lesson-03.md) — one take, the watch-back rubric, think-aloud and zero help, how many strangers.
- [15.4 · When the demo breaks](../lessons/module-15/lesson-04.md) — failure classes, P(clean), live vs recorded vs pre-baked, the recovery pattern.

**Pass mark:** 70% (7 of 10). Unlimited retries; answers and explanations appear after you submit.

Before you start, it helps to have done the labs in [`labs/module-15`](../labs/module-15/README.md) — several questions use `DemoCheck` output from the assembled demo repository and its breaks.
