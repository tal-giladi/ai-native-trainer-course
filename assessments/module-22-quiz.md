# Module 22 quiz — Productization and scaling

Nine scenario questions on turning consulting into a practice that is not only hours: service cards whose hours fall with repetition, formats that carry different evidence, licences that answer the questions buyers will ask, kits and assessments treated as versioned products, and a capacity-capped plan with retainers that cannot become debts. They test reasoning, not recall: most describe a card, a catalog, a kit, an item analysis or a plan and ask what is wrong or what to do next.

**Covers**

- [22.1 · From hours to repeatable service](../lessons/module-22/lesson-01.md) — consulting vs productized service vs product, the service card, custom steps, effective day rate, Wright's learning curve and delivery variability.
- [22.2 · Workshop to course to community](../lessons/module-22/lesson-02.md) — what each format carries, completion, revenue per live day, licence layers and team-licence terms (orientation only), third-party material, community rules.
- [22.3 · Templates, kits and assessments as products](../lessons/module-22/lesson-03.md) — versions, changelog, LICENSE files, support, stranger tests with Wilson intervals, deny-list scans, item difficulty and discrimination, certificates vs credentials.
- [22.4 · Capacity-limited consulting and the productization plan](../lessons/module-22/lesson-04.md) — the cap and allocation, capped and expiring retainers, utilization and waiting (Kingman), revenue ranges, exit signals and stop rules.

**Pass mark:** 70% (7 of 9). Unlimited retries; answers and explanations appear after you submit.

Before you start, it helps to have run the checks in [`labs/module-22`](../labs/module-22/README.md) on the break files.
