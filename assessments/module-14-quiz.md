# Module 14 quiz — Naming your method

Nine scenario questions on turning your own evidence into a teachable method: telling a method from a tool tutorial, mining incidents into named concepts, giving each concept the status its evidence supports, versioning the method, and crediting what you borrowed. They test reasoning, not recall: most describe a draft, a concept card, a change or a claim and ask what is wrong with it or what to do next.

**Covers**

- [14.1 · Why vocabulary is the IP](../lessons/module-14/lesson-01.md) — the seven parts of a methodology, names as handles, semantic diffusion, the tool-change test.
- [14.2 · Incident to principle to name](../lessons/module-14/lesson-02.md) — clustering by mechanism, bounded falsifiable claims, evidence strength and status, name tests, the paraphrase test.
- [14.3 · Diagrams, versioning and the evolution policy](../lessons/module-14/lesson-03.md) — diagram rules, semantic versioning for a method, retire-never-delete, evolution triggers, the objections FAQ.
- [14.4 · Originality, attribution and intellectual property](../lessons/module-14/lesson-04.md) — what copyright and trademark do not cover, original synthesis vs copied framework, attribute / replace / justify, employer material.

**Pass mark:** 70% (7 of 9). Unlimited retries; answers and explanations appear after you submit.

Before you start, it helps to have run the four checks in [`labs/module-14`](../labs/module-14/README.md) on the break files.
