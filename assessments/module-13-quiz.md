# Module 13 quiz — Measuring AI engineering impact

Ten scenario questions on measuring whether coding agents changed an engineering team's delivery: choosing metrics, reading published studies, designing a comparison, spotting threats to validity, analyzing skewed data and reporting honestly. They test reasoning, not recall: most describe a claim, a data set or an analysis and ask what it shows, what is wrong with it, or what to do next.

**Covers**

- [13.1 · Delivery metrics](../lessons/module-13/lesson-01.md) — clocks with start and stop events, skewed durations, guardrails, cost per successful task.
- [13.2 · The evidence base on AI productivity](../lessons/module-13/lesson-02.md) — what the RCTs, field studies and surveys found, why they disagree, critiquing claims.
- [13.3 · Experiment design](../lessons/module-13/lesson-03.md) — control arms, randomization within developer × size, sample size, pre-registration.
- [13.4 · Threats to validity](../lessons/module-13/lesson-04.md) — selection, regression to the mean, contamination, version changes, difference-in-differences.
- [13.5 · Statistics for engineering comparisons](../lessons/module-13/lesson-05.md) — log scale, bootstrap, permutation tests, effect sizes, practical vs statistical significance.
- [13.6 · Running and reporting the comparison](../lessons/module-13/lesson-06.md) — fixed duration, ticket flow, the claims ladder, money as a range.

**Pass mark:** 70% (7 of 10). Unlimited retries; answers and explanations appear after you submit.

Before you start, it helps to have done the labs in [`labs/module-13`](../labs/module-13/README.md) — several questions use `ImpactStats` output from the illustrative Contoso data sets.
