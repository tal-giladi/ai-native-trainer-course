# Module 20 quiz — Offers, pricing, selling and consulting

Nine scenario questions on selling honestly: positioning and an offer ladder in which each rung earns the next, prices built from your own measured interval, qualification and discovery calls without pressure, proposals and SOWs that replace promises with measurements, and the employer, contract and client situations that end careers when ignored. They test reasoning, not recall: most describe a ladder, a worksheet, a call, a SOW or a request from a client and ask what is wrong or what to do next.

**Covers**

- [20.1 · Positioning and the offer ladder](../lessons/module-20/lesson-01.md) — jobs to be done, the five-slot positioning sentence, evidence-producing rungs, honest capacity vs invented scarcity, decoys.
- [20.2 · Pricing with uncertainty](../lessons/module-20/lesson-02.md) — cost floor, market reference and value ceiling; pricing models; value and ROI as intervals from your own effect; corners vs Monte Carlo; pricing a pilot as a measurement.
- [20.3 · Qualification and discovery calls](../lessons/module-20/lesson-03.md) — application forms and data minimization, call/nurture/refer/decline, the discovery call, expectation resets, pressure tactics.
- [20.4 · Proposals, SOWs and scope control](../lessons/module-20/lesson-04.md) — proposals in the client's words, results-based deliverables and acceptance criteria, exclusions, change control, measurement instead of a guarantee, procurement.
- [20.5 · Legal, admin and difficult clients](../lessons/module-20/lesson-05.md) — employer clauses and permission, IP, contractor status, payment terms, the admin register, difficult-client patterns (orientation only, jurisdiction-dependent).

**Pass mark:** 70% (7 of 9). Unlimited retries; answers and explanations appear after you submit.

Before you start, it helps to have run the checks in [`labs/module-20`](../labs/module-20/README.md) on the break files.
