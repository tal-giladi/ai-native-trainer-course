# Module 1 quiz — The AI-native trainer role and picking your wedge

Nine scenario questions on choosing what job you are training for, telling engineering pain from AI hype, picking a wedge and running discovery interviews that produce evidence instead of politeness. They test reasoning, not recall. Most describe a request, a plan, a wedge or an interview and ask what it shows, what is wrong with it, or what to do next.

**Covers**

- [01.1 · The roles and the flywheel](../lessons/module-01/lesson-01.md) — trainer, consultant, enablement engineer; the four-stage flywheel; the proof chain; stage skipping.
- [01.2 · Engineering pain vs AI hype](../lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md) — four tests and the workaround signal; symptoms and blockers; buyer, users, champion; sizing effort vs flow.
- [01.3 · Finding your wedge](../lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md) — stack × audience × pain; five criteria; anchoring on the stack, not the tool; reachability.
- [01.4 · Customer discovery interviews](../lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md) — the evidence ladder, question defects, how many interviews, keep / change / kill.

**Pass mark:** 70% (7 of 9). Unlimited retries; answers and explanations appear after you submit.

Several questions use the lab transcript and worksheets in [`labs/module-01`](../labs/module-01/README.md); it helps to have critiqued the bad interview first.
