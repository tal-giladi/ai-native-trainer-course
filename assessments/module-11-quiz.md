# Module 11 quiz — Agents in CI and production

Nine scenario questions on running agents unattended: headless run contracts, CI review measured against humans, recurring automation, operating agent jobs, and governance of the AI layer. They test reasoning, not recall — most describe a workflow, a result or a month of numbers and ask what it shows or what to change.

**Covers**

- [11.1 · Headless agents](../lessons/module-11/lesson-01.md) — the run contract, result subtypes and permission denials, untrusted triggers and secrets.
- [11.2 · CI review: signal vs noise](../lessons/module-11/lesson-02.md) — precision, recall, the base-rate ceiling, ground truth, dev/holdout tuning.
- [11.3 · Recurring automation](../lessons/module-11/lesson-03.md) — net value and break-even acceptance, propose/validate/apply, idempotency, scheduling.
- [11.4 · Operating agents: approval, rollback, observability and cost](../lessons/module-11/lesson-04.md) — approval by reversibility, rollback and kill switch, the ledger, ceilings, retries by failure class.
- [11.5 · Governance of the AI layer](../lessons/module-11/lesson-05.md) — owners, change classes, the system-evolution loop, cadence and overrides.

**Pass mark:** 70% (7 of 9). Unlimited retries; answers and explanations appear after you submit.

Before you start, do the labs in [`labs/module-11`](../labs/module-11/README.md): several questions use `AgentOps` output from the review data set, the triage samples and the run ledger.
