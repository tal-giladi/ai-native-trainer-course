# Module 21 quiz — Delivering engagements

Nine scenario questions on running an engagement from kickoff to case study: who to map and interview before kickoff, what a baseline must be and what a small engagement can detect, how to build an AI layer so the client team owns it, how to report results and hand over so they hold after you leave, and how to write a case study that neither identifies the client nor claims more than the record. They test reasoning, not recall: most describe a record, a PR log, a result or a draft and ask what is wrong or what to do next.

**Covers**

- [21.1 · Discovery, kickoff and stakeholder mapping](../lessons/module-21/lesson-01.md) — past-event interviews, the five stakeholder groups, acceptors, dated access, premortem, measurement instead of targets.
- [21.2 · Technical audit and baseline](../lessons/module-21/lesson-02.md) — evidence rungs on the client's before state, baseline timing and shape, guardrails, the minimum detectable effect.
- [21.3 · Architecture and build with the team](../lessons/module-21/lesson-03.md) — recommend vs decide, the fading scaffold, client-authored share, client truck factor.
- [21.4 · Enable, measure, hand over, follow up](../lessons/module-21/lesson-04.md) — intervals and statuses, "what this does not show", owners who ran it without you, 30/60/90-day follow-ups.
- [21.5 · Writing the anonymized case study](../lessons/module-21/lesson-05.md) — tracing numbers to the record, identifiability and bands, typical results, written approval.

**Pass mark:** 70% (7 of 9). Unlimited retries; answers and explanations appear after you submit.

Before you start, it helps to have run `EngageCheck` on the break files in [`labs/module-21`](../labs/module-21/README.md).
