# Module 17 quiz — Workshop design and delivery

Nine scenario questions on turning a method, a demo repository and a short session into a rehearsed 2-hour hands-on workshop: laying out the timed arc, cutting the demo into blocks with hands-on steps, building the materials that absorb room failures, answering hard questions with evidence, and judging readiness from rehearsal logs. They test reasoning, not recall: most describe an agenda, a kit, an answer or a set of rehearsal results and ask what is wrong or what to do next.

**Covers**

- [17.1 · The workshop agenda](../lessons/module-17/lesson-01.md) — the six-part arc, show-do blocks on sibling tickets, wait-time plans, timestamps, buffer, break, close, cut order, cue-card notes.
- [17.2 · Workshop materials](../lessons/module-17/lesson-02.md) — starter pack, setup check, offline and no-agent paths, catch-up tags, exercise specs, troubleshooting from evidence, the room's rate limit, fallbacks and forms.
- [17.3 · The hard-questions bank](../lessons/module-17/lesson-03.md) — categories, the answer shape, evidence order, the claims ladder in Q&A, wait-time triage.
- [17.4 · Rehearsal protocol](../lessons/module-17/lesson-04.md) — three rehearsals with different jobs, drift decomposition, the observation rubric and observer agreement, the exit standard.

**Pass mark:** 70% (7 of 9). Unlimited retries; answers and explanations appear after you submit.

Before you start, it helps to have run the checks in [`labs/module-17`](../labs/module-17/README.md) on the break files.
