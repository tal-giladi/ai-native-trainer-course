### C-20261018-01 · AI assistance and skill formation: learning-outcome RCTs (Anthropic 2026, Bastani et al. PNAS 2025)

- **Class:** B
- **Kind:** new-topic
- **Date discovered:** 2026-10-18 (logged as class C in daily/2026-10-18.md; reclassified B by the 2026-W42 weekly review)
- **Date published:** 2026-01-29 (Anthropic); 2025 (PNAS 122(26))
- **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20245 and https://www.anthropic.com/research/AI-assistance-coding-skills (both fetched 2026-10-18); https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122 (abstract-level figures via search results; publisher unreachable)
- **Organization/researchers:** Judy Hanwen Shen and Alex Tamkin (Anthropic); Hamsa Bastani, Osbert Bastani, Alp Sungu, Haosen Ge, Özge Kabakcı, Rei Mariman (Wharton/UPenn and others)
- **Category:** productivity-evidence
- **What changed:** Two RCTs measure what learners can do without the tool after learning with it. Shen and Tamkin: 52 mostly junior developers learning Trio; quiz 50% (AI) vs 67%, d = 0.738, p = 0.01, biggest gap on debugging, ~2 min faster not significant; delegation-style patterns <40%, conceptual-inquiry/explanation patterns ≥65%. Bastani et al.: ~1,000 high-school students; plain GPT-4 practice +48% but exam −17%; safeguarded tutor practice +127%, exam drop largely mitigated.
- **Technical summary:** Speed and competence are separate outcomes; unstructured AI help during learning can reduce independent skill, and the interaction pattern (explain/ask vs delegate) matters.
- **Why it might matter:** 13.2's evidence base measures only speed, throughput and quality; a trainer must also answer "what happens to juniors' skills" and design workshop exercises so the agent does not do the learning for participants.
- **Evidence of adoption:** n/a (research); Anthropic study is vendor-run (vendor claim for its summary), Bastani et al. is independent and peer-reviewed; coverage by InfoQ, DevClass, and a critical independent review (jenniferplusplus.com).
- **Major organizations using it:** n/a
- **Open-source implementation:** none
- **Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20245, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122
- **Code:** none
- **Relationship to existing course material:** lessons/module-13/lesson-02.md (evidence table, "Why they disagree"); lessons/module-16/lesson-03.md (show-do-reflect, desirable difficulties); no registry topic.
- **Potential course lesson:** two rows + short subsection in 13.2; no new lesson.
- **Confidence:** medium (small n and short-term outcomes; but two independent trials agree in direction)
