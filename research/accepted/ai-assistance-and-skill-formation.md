## Summary

Randomized trials that measure what people can do *without* the tool after learning *with* it. Shen and Tamkin (Anthropic, 2026) found developers learning an unfamiliar library with a chat assistant scored 50% vs 67% on a follow-up quiz with no significant speed gain, and that delegation-style use scored lowest. Bastani et al. (PNAS 2025) found the same shape in high-school maths: plain chat raised practice scores and cut exam scores 17%, a safeguarded tutor largely removed the drop.
