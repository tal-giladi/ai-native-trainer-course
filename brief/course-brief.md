# Course Brief

## Topic

AI-Native Trainer — from coding-agent power-user to the person engineering organizations pay to design, secure, measure, teach and deliver AI-native software development.

## Source

- `ai-native-trainer-path.html` — career-path write-up built from a teardown of a paid "make your entire development cycle AI-native" workshop and the trainer business behind it.
- Revision comments (2026-09-28) — expand to 18–22 modules / ~100 lessons / 20–30 h of instruction; add LLM fundamentals, context engineering, agent evaluation, agent security, multi-agent systems, enterprise architecture, rigorous measurement, instructional design, change management, productization; build as a static course website with simulations, quizzes, templates, glossary, research log and maintenance plan.

## Target Audience

An experienced C#/.NET engineer and technical leader, fluent in GitHub, SQL Server, JavaScript, APIs, Docker, cloud, architecture, security concepts, and daily coding-agent use. No basic programming is taught.

## Desired Outcome

The graduate can walk into a real engineering organization, inspect its development workflow and AI-agent architecture, find the weaknesses, design an improved system, evaluate it, measure its effect honestly, secure it, explain it to engineers, train the team, and sell and deliver the engagement professionally — with an original, evidence-backed method.

## Duration

22 modules, ~101 lessons, ~26 hours of instruction, ~75–100 hours of hands-on and field work over ~6–9 months.

## Special instructions

Preserve the original philosophy (practitioner before teacher, evidence before price, own it don't resell it, tool-agnostic core, real reps, honest limits, security/IP first-class, every module ships an artifact, portfolio over completion). Going solo, end to end.
