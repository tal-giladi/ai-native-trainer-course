# Statement of work (SOW)

The document that says exactly what you will deliver, how each deliverable is accepted, what is excluded, what the client must provide, how changes are handled, how much is paid and when. Introduced in [20.4 · Proposals, SOWs and scope control](../lessons/module-20/lesson-04.md). Check with `OfferCheck sow`; log scope requests with `OfferCheck changes` ([Module 20 labs](../labs/module-20/README.md)).

> [!CAUTION]
> Orientation only. This template gives structure, not legal wording. Contract law, IP, liability, tax and worker-status rules depend on your jurisdiction and the client's. Have the legal clauses (sections 12–14 and any master agreement) written or reviewed by a lawyer where you work, and read your employment contract before you sign anything ([20.5](../lessons/module-20/lesson-05.md)).

## Rules

- **Results, not hours.** Describe each deliverable by what will be true and how a third person could check it, in the spirit of performance-based work statements (FAR 37.602): required results and measurable standards, not "how" or "how many hours".
- **Every deliverable has an acceptance criterion** someone other than you and the client could verify. "To the client's satisfaction" is not a criterion.
- **Exclusions are listed**, especially the things the client will assume are included.
- **Measurement, not a guarantee.** Outcomes are measured against a baseline and reported with intervals, including null or negative results.
- **Changes in writing**, with their effect on fee and schedule, before work on them starts.
- **Fees add up.** The payment schedule sums to the total.

## Scope-request log (keep it from day one)

| Date | Request | In SOW | Decision | CR | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD | [what was asked, by whom (role)] | yes / no | done / agreed / declined / deferred / absorbed | CR-nn or - | [fee and schedule effect, or "none"] |

Every "no" in the *In SOW* column ends as a change request, a logged free concession confirmed in writing, a deferral or a decline. Never as silent work.
