# Attribution audit template (`method-notes/attribution-audit.md`)

Every term, loop, diagram and example in your method that came from someone else, and what you did about it. Introduced in [14.4](../lessons/module-14/lesson-04.md). Rerun before every public release and whenever you read a new framework in your space.

> [!NOTE]
> This is an honesty and credibility check, not legal advice. Copyright, trademark and employment law differ by country; for anything you will sell or register, ask a lawyer in your jurisdiction.
