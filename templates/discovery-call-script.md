# Discovery-call script

A 30-minute call with a prospect who has applied (see the [application form](application-form.md)) and may want to buy. Its job is to find out, honestly and together, whether you can help, how the decision will be made, and what a sensible next step is — including "not me". Introduced in [20.3 · Qualification and discovery calls](../lessons/module-20/lesson-03.md).

This is the sales counterpart of the [customer discovery script](customer-discovery-script.md) from Module 1. The question rules are the same (past events, not opinions; listen more than you talk); what is new is that you will, at the end, say whether and how you can help, and at what price.

## Rules

1. **Their problem first.** No offer, method or price before about minute 20. If they ask early, answer in one sentence and come back to them.
2. **You speak less than half the words**; aim for about 40%. `OfferCheck call` warns above 45%.
3. **No pressure tactics.** No invented scarcity, deadlines, price-rise threats, bandwagon claims or "sign today". If you have a real capacity cap or a real start date, state it once, as a fact.
4. **No promises of outcomes.** Say what you measured, with its interval and its population, and offer to measure theirs.
5. **Say a limit out loud.** What you cannot promise, what you do not do, or that they do not need you. It is the sentence that makes the rest believable.
6. **End with a dated next step** both of you agreed to, or with a clear no.

## Before the call

- [ ] Application read; score from `OfferCheck qualify`; flags (expectation, conflict) noted.
- [ ] Consent for notes (and recording, only if you will use it) confirmed in writing.
- [ ] Your offer ladder and prices open, so you do not improvise a price.
- [ ] One sentence ready for your own evidence, with its interval and what it does not show.

## The call (30 minutes)

| Min | Section | Questions (pick, don't recite) | You are listening for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Open | "Before anything about what I do, can you tell me about the team and what it's working on?" | Size, stack, who they report to |
| 2–10 | The last time | "Tell me about the last time [the problem on the form] happened." · "What happened next?" · "What did it cost?" · "How do you know?" | A dated, costed event; how they measure today |
| 10–13 | Tried so far | "What have you tried?" · "What happened to that?" | Workarounds, abandoned tools, what they will compare you with |
| 13–15 | Success | "If this were solved, how would you know? What number would move?" | Measures you can put in a SOW |
| 15–20 | Decision | "Who decides on something like this, and who signs?" · "Is there a budget line, or would it come from somewhere else?" · "By when do you need to know?" · "What would make it impossible — access, security, people's time?" | Sponsor, procurement path, budget, deadline, constraints |
| 20–26 | Fit | Say which rung fits and why, or that none does. Say what it would and would not show. Say the price from your ladder. | Their reaction to the limit, not only to the price |
| 26–30 | Next step | "Next step: I send [X] by [date], we review it on [date]. Does that work?" · "Is there anything I should have asked?" | A date both agreed to |

## Expectation resets (say them early, once, plainly)

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Can you guarantee 30%?" | "No, and I'd be wary of anyone who does. My measured result was [effect, interval, population]. I can measure it on your tickets and report the range, even if it's zero." |
| "We want to cut headcount." | "That isn't what I help with, and I wouldn't measure it. If the goal is fewer PRs failing review, I can help." |
| "Can we start before the contract is signed?" | "I'd rather not, for both our sakes. If the SOW is signed by [date], we start on [date]." |
| "Just do it for us." | "I can build it with your team; built for them, it tends not to survive my leaving." (Module 19) |

## After the call (same day)

- [ ] Notes: the incident in their words (quotes), decision path, budget, deadline, constraints, measures they named.
- [ ] Run `OfferCheck call` on the transcript or notes; read every warning.
- [ ] Send what you promised by the date you promised. If it is a proposal, use the [proposal template](proposal.md).
- [ ] If it is not a fit, say so in writing within two working days, with a pointer elsewhere.
